## About

StreamDock is a centralized, community-driven directory that curates the best free streaming websites for movies, TV shows, and entertainment. We aim to provide a safe, organized, and high-quality browsing experience, helping you discover free content across various regions and platforms without the hassle of endless searching.

## Features

- 🌐 **Curated Directory:** A vast list of verified websites for free entertainment content.
- 🌍 **Regional Content:** Filter content by region to find platforms available in your area.
- 🔍 **Easy Search:** Quickly find your favorite entertainment sites.
- 🛡️ **Community Trusted:** Content curated to ensure a safer and cleaner browsing experience.
- 🚀 **Lightning Fast:** Optimized, lightweight interface for quick access.
